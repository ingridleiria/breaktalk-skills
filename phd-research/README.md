# PhD research track

Twenty-eight skills covering the empirical research pipeline from a vague topic to a replication package, and beyond it into examining and refereeing, written for doctoral students and early career researchers in economics and the quantitative social sciences.

The standard they enforce is the one a referee applies. A design is written before anything is estimated. A citation does not enter a draft until it has been checked against a real record. Causal language is calibrated to what the design can support, and a null result is reported as a result. None of this is difficult; all of it is skipped under deadline, which is why it belongs in an instruction file rather than in good intentions.

## How they chain

The track is a loop, not a list. Each stage produces the input the next one needs.

- **Before any data**: research-question-ideation, research-design, literature-verification, theoretical-framework-review
- **Data and code**: data-profiling-and-cleaning, stata-project-scaffold
- **Estimation and defence**: econometric-model-writer, identification-defense
- **Exhibits**: descriptive-statistics-tables, academic-figures-monochrome, academic-tables-booktabs
- **The manuscript**: full-manuscript-build to run the whole thing, then introduction-writer, data-section-writer, results-writing, discussion-and-conclusion, abstract-and-title, references-and-bibliography
- **Getting it published**: journal-targeting, peer-review-simulator, response-to-reviewers
- **Talking about it**: conference-presentation-deck, thesis-defense-prep, research-proposal-and-grant
- **The thesis itself**: thesis-advisor for direction and triage, thesis-chapter-review for an examiner's reading
- **The other side of review**: refereeing-for-a-journal, for when you are the one asked to review
- **Closing it out**: replication-package

Design feeds the model writer and the identification defence. The section writers draw on literature-verification, so nothing unverified reaches the draft. The peer review simulator runs before journal targeting, because the objections change which journal is realistic. Two skills sit deliberately apart from the pipeline: thesis-advisor takes a supervisor's position on what to do next rather than judging the work, and refereeing-for-a-journal is for the reverse direction, when the paper on your desk is somebody else's.

## Figures and tables

The exhibit skills assume print. Figures are monochrome by default, with series separated by marker, dash pattern, and texture rather than colour, and the legend outside the plot area. Tables use three rules and no vertical lines. Both survive photocopying, greyscale printing, and a referee reading on a phone.
