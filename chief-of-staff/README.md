# Chief of Staff track

Forty-four skills for the operating layer of a company: what the role actually is at each stage and how to do it there, then the memo, the model, the board, the plan, the raise, the cadence, and the commercial document. Written for whoever holds the work between the executive and the function, which in a small firm is one person and in a larger one is a team.

The through line is that quality here depends on discipline rather than inspiration. A market analysis is good because every number has a source. A programme status is good because it names one owner and a dated event rather than a percentage. That kind of quality is exactly what written instructions can enforce, so these skills enforce it.

## How they chain

Research feeds decisions, decisions feed documents, documents feed the operating rhythm.

- **Know what the job is**: chief-of-staff-by-stage first, then the playbook for where your company actually is, cos-at-seed, cos-at-series-a, cos-at-series-b, cos-at-series-c, or cos-at-series-d. Each one acts as the role at that stage: the first ninety days, what to build, what to decide alone, what breaks at that size, and the signals that the stage has ended
- **Think and structure**: structured-problem-solving, which is the method the rest of the track assumes, then market-research, external-insights, competitive-battlecard, customer-interview-synthesis
- **Decide and plan**: decision-memo, ideation-deck, strategic-plan-and-action-plan, okr-planning, annual-planning-and-headcount, operating-cadence-design, partnership-assessment, vendor-evaluation
- **Model the money**: financial-model-builder, pricing-and-resourcing-model, revenue-forecast, pipeline-deep-dive
- **Raise and govern**: fundraise-readiness, pitch-deck, board-and-investor-management, board-deck, investor-update
- **Win and paper the work**: outreach-email, tailored-client-deck, pitch-deck, proposal-writer, sow-and-scope
- **Run the company**: program-management, weekly-status-update, meeting-to-decisions, process-documentation-sop, hiring-scorecard-and-interview-kit, onboarding-plan, event-and-offsite-planning
- **Communicate upward and outward**: executive-briefing, ceo-communications, crisis-and-incident-comms, principal-simulator
- **Change how the company works**: ai-adoption-program, process-documentation-sop, operating-cadence-design

Several of them hand off directly. The forecast reads the pipeline deep dive. Pricing uses the structure the financial model builder produces. The statement of work inherits the deliverables the proposal promised, and says so. The strategic plan hands its objectives to okr-planning, its people and money to annual-planning-and-headcount, and its rhythm to operating-cadence-design. Board work runs board-and-investor-management around board-deck, since the deck is the visible part and the pre-wiring is what decides the meeting. Anything going upward can be run past principal-simulator before it is sent.

## A note on stage

The role is not one job. A Chief of Staff at twenty people builds the first version of everything; at eighty they rebuild what has broken; at three hundred they synthesise across a leadership team that now exists; beyond that they act in place of the CEO within an agreed scope. Which skills in this track matter, and which are somebody else's job, depends on which of those you are. [chief-of-staff-by-stage](chief-of-staff-by-stage/SKILL.md) works out which one you are in, including what to stop doing at each transition, which is the part nobody announces. The five stage playbooks then act as the role there, and they are deliberately different from one another rather than one document with the numbers changed.
