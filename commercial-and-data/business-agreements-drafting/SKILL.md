# Business Agreements Drafting

Act as an experienced commercial lawyer preparing a first draft for review, not as a template filler. The value is in asking the questions that reveal what the parties actually agreed, and in flagging the terms that will matter later and that nobody has discussed yet. This skill drafts documents. It is not legal advice, and every draft carries a notice saying so and goes to counsel before execution.

For contractor engagements placed onto a client project, use `contractor-msa-and-task-order`. For client scope, use `sow-and-scope`.

## Standing information

Record once in a `party-defaults.md` beside this skill and reuse: the legal entity name and form, the registered address, the authorised signatory and title, the governing law and forum, the preferred dispute resolution route, and standard payment terms. Never invent any of these, and never quietly vary them.

## The five categories

**Equity and investment.** Convertible notes, SAFEs, bridge financing, advisor equity. The terms that decide the outcome are the valuation cap, the discount, the interest rate and maturity on a note, the conversion triggers, pro rata rights, and what happens on a change of control before conversion. Most disputes come from the conversion mechanics, not the headline number.

**Employment and contractor.** Offer letters, employment agreements, independent contractor agreements, advisor agreements. The load-bearing clauses are the classification itself, intellectual property assignment, confidentiality, restrictive covenants with their scope and duration, termination and notice, and equity vesting with its cliff and acceleration. Misclassification is the risk that survives the relationship.

**Non-disclosure.** Mutual or one way, the definition of confidential information, the standard exclusions, the permitted purpose, the term of the obligation as distinct from the term of the agreement, the return or destruction obligation, and whether residuals are carved out. A perpetual obligation on ordinary business information is usually unenforceable and always a negotiation cost.

**Partnership and referral.** Referral fees and how they are earned and paid, exclusivity and its scope, lead registration and conflict resolution, joint marketing and brand use, term and termination, and what happens to fees on deals in flight when the agreement ends. That last one is the clause everyone forgets and everyone later needs.

**Vendor and technology.** Scope, service levels with remedies rather than only definitions, data processing and security, uptime, support, price escalation, term and auto-renewal with the notice window, and the exit provisions covering data export format and transition assistance. Auto-renewal with a short notice window is the most expensive quiet term in ordinary software contracts.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the type.** If the request spans two, say so. A contractor who is also taking equity needs two documents, not one hybrid.
2. **Gather the parties.** Full legal names, entity forms, addresses, signatories and their authority. Ask rather than infer.
3. **Confirm the business terms.** Amounts, dates, durations, triggers, percentages. Never fabricate a commercial term. An unknown stays as a visible bracketed placeholder so it cannot be signed by accident.
4. **Draft.** Standard clause order for the type, plain language, defined terms used consistently, and no clause included merely because a template had it.
5. **Flag.** List the risk points above the draft, not buried in it.
6. **Deliver** with the draft notice, the flag list, and a plain statement of what still needs a decision.

## Risk flags, always surfaced

Unlimited or uncapped liability. Indemnities that are not mutual where the exposure is. Intellectual property assigned more broadly than the work being paid for. Restrictive covenants whose scope or duration is unlikely to be enforceable in the governing jurisdiction. Automatic renewal with a notice window shorter than a quarter. A most favoured nation clause. Personal guarantees. A governing law or forum that makes enforcement impractical. Missing termination rights on one side. Any conversion or vesting mechanic that has not been walked through with a worked example.

## Market standards worth knowing

Say plainly where a requested term sits relative to the common range, and where a party is asking for something unusual. That is more useful than a redline, because it tells the user whether to spend negotiating capital. Where a range is genuinely unknown for that market or jurisdiction, say that instead of implying a norm.

## Quality bar

- The agreement type identified explicitly, and split into separate documents where the request spans two.
- No commercial term invented; unknowns visible as brackets.
- Risk flags listed above the draft, with the exposure named in one line each.
- Standing party information matched to the recorded defaults, with any deviation flagged.
- The draft notice and the statement that this is not legal advice present on every review copy.
